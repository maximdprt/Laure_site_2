A bottle on a table, a label, a few drops. From the outside, a personalised blend looks much like any other. Yet each one is different. It is not picked from a shelf: it is built, step by step, around the person it is made for.

How do you go from a conversation and a few chosen colours to a unique bottle? This article walks you through the process, step by step.

## Why a unique blend?

We do not all react in the same way to life's situations. The same situation can bring up fear in one person, stress in another, or anger or discouragement in someone else. A period of change can feel like a release to one person and like a loss of bearings to another.

That is why there is no single blend for everyone. Offering the same bottle to two people going through a similar situation would mean ignoring what makes them unique: their story, their personality, the way they feel things.

> Your profile shapes your support.

This approach takes time and attentive listening. It means taking an interest in the person as a whole, not only in the situation that brings them. That is also what gives the bottle its meaning: it is not chosen for you, it is thought through with you.

A personalised blend therefore always starts with you. With what you are living, what you would like to work on, and what your Chromatic Profile brings to light.

## The four families of essences

A personalised blend can combine several families of essences. This is the Chromobioénergie® method, which uses the properties of colours and sounds combined with flowers, plants and crystals.

### Flowers

Flower essences have traditionally been used to accompany different emotional states. They are often associated with work around self-confidence, fears, stress, change or letting go. This is the best-known family, often referred to as floral therapy.

### Colours

Colours are at the heart of the method. Each colour has its own characteristics. Your profile helps identify the colours that hold a particular place in your personal balance, the ones that can support you through the period you are going through.

### Sounds

Some essences are associated with sound frequencies. They complement the work done with colours and flowers, within a holistic approach to the person.

### Crystals

Crystal essences can also be part of your blend. They are used within a well-being and harmonising approach.

These four families are not always all present. The composition depends entirely on your profile and your needs.

## From profile to bottle

Creating a personalised blend follows a simple path that leaves plenty of room for listening.

### 1. The conversation

Everything starts with time to talk. We take stock of your situation, what brings you and what you would like to see change. You don't need a specific issue: you can also come simply because you would like to know yourself better.

### 2. The Chromatic Profile Test

You then choose different colours according to your attraction or your feelings. There is no right or wrong answer. Your choices establish your Chromatic Profile, which we look at together.

### 3. Reading the profile

The profile brings certain emotional areas to light: present needs, resources, sometimes tensions. This shared reading becomes the guiding thread for what follows.

### 4. Selecting the elements

From your profile, the elements best suited to your support are selected from flowers, colours, sounds and crystals. Each choice has a reason, linked to what emerged from the test and to your goal.

### 5. Creating the bottle

Your blend is then made specifically for you. If you came to the practice in Lacanau, it can be handed to you directly. If the test took place remotely, it is sent to you by post.

## A blend that evolves with you

Your first blend reflects your profile and your situation at the time of the test. But life moves on, and so do your needs. What was in the foreground a few weeks ago may have given way to other questions.

That is why your blend can be renewed or adjusted. A follow-up session lets you take stock of your journey and, if needed, adapt the composition. If you have already taken your test and simply wish to renew or adjust your bottle, this can be done without a new full session.

The aim is to support you as closely as possible along your journey, taking into account what changes.

## What about the themed bottles?

If you would like to start more simply, ready-made themed bottles are also available: serenity, self-confidence, letting go, restful nights, managing emotions, energy, protection, replenishment. They are designed to accompany a present need.

They do not, however, replace a personalised blend. For a bottle fully tailored to your profile, the starting point remains the Chromatic Profile Test.

## In short

A personalised blend is not a standard product: it is the continuation of a conversation and of a careful reading of your profile. Flowers, colours, sounds and crystals are combined according to your needs, within a well-being and self-knowledge approach. And because you change, your blend can change with you.
