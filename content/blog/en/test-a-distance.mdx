Do you live far from Lacanau, have a busy schedule, or simply feel more at ease at home? The Chromatic Profile Test can be taken remotely, wherever you are in France. The approach is the same as at the practice: only the format changes.

Here is, step by step, how a remote test works.

## Who is it for?

The remote test is for anyone who would like to discover their Chromatic Profile without travelling. It is particularly suitable if:

- you live outside Gironde or far from the Médoc;
- you have little time to travel;
- you prefer to talk from a familiar place;
- you have already met Laure at the practice and wish to continue remotely.

As at the practice, you don't need a specific issue. You can take the test because you are going through a period of change or questioning, or simply because you would like to know yourself better.

## Step 1: booking

Everything starts with booking your time slot online. You choose the service (the Chromatic Profile Test alone, or the Test + personalised blend package), then the "remote" format.

When booking, you give your postal address. It is used to send you your colour cards before the session. This is why a few days are allowed between booking and the appointment: time for the post to reach you.

A deposit is paid online to confirm your appointment. It is deducted from the price of the session. You immediately receive a confirmation email with all the details.

### Why five days?

For a remote test, bookings are made at least five days in advance. This is not an administrative constraint: it ensures your colour cards reach you in good time, without any rush. You can then approach the session with peace of mind, with everything you need to hand.

If something unexpected comes up, you can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 48 hours before, by contacting Laure by email or phone. After that, the deposit is kept.

### Practice or remote: how to choose?

Both formats offer the same support. The practice in Lacanau offers an in-person meeting, and your blend can be handed to you directly. The remote format saves you a journey and lets you experience the session in your own surroundings. The right choice is simply the one that suits you best.

## Step 2: receiving your colour cards

A few days before the session, you receive your colour cards, by post or as a PDF. They contain the colours you will be invited to choose during the test.

There is nothing special to prepare. Simply keep your cards close at hand, without trying to "think" about your choices in advance. The test relies on your spontaneous feelings during the session.

### A few practical tips

- Settle somewhere quiet where you won't be disturbed.
- If possible, sit in natural light to see the colours clearly.
- Keep something to write with, if you like to keep a record of the conversation.
- Make sure your phone is charged.

## Step 3: the test by phone

At the agreed time, Laure calls you. The session unfolds exactly as it would at the practice.

### The conversation

You begin by taking stock together of your situation and of what brings you. This time of listening is essential: it helps to understand what you are living and what you would like to work on.

### Choosing the colours

You then choose different colours on your cards according to your attraction or your feelings, and share them as the conversation goes on. There is no right or wrong answer: what matters is your personal perception.

### Reading your profile

Your choices establish your Chromatic Profile. Together, you look at what it brings to light: your emotions, your present needs, your resources, certain blocks, your aspirations.

There is no set length. The session takes the time you need.

> The remote test is not a simplified version: it is the same support, by another route.

## Step 4: receiving your blend

If you chose the Test + personalised blend package, your bottle is created from your profile after the session. Depending on your needs, it combines flower, colour, sound and crystal essences.

Your blend is then sent to you by post, with the information you need to use it.

Later on, if your needs evolve, you can book a follow-up session by phone, or request the renewal or adjustment of your blend directly from the shop.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the remote test as complete as at the practice?

Yes. The conversation, the colour choices and the reading of the profile are identical. Only the medium changes: you hold it in your hands at home.

### Do I need a video connection?

No. The test takes place by phone. All you need are your colour cards and a quiet place.

### Can I ask my questions first?

Of course. A free fifteen-minute discovery call lets you get acquainted and ask your questions before booking.

## In short

The remote Chromatic Profile Test follows four simple steps: booking with your postal address, receiving your colour cards, the test by phone, and then, if you wish, receiving your personalised blend. A way to discover your profile without travelling, within a well-being and self-knowledge approach.
